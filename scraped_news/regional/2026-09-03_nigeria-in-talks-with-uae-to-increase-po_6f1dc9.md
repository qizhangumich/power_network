# Nigeria in talks with UAE to increase power generation
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/energy/2026/09/nigeria-in-talks-with-uae-to-increase-power-generation/

Nigeria is in talks with the UAE to increase its electricity generation capacity by 5 gigawatts. Solar energy is among the main areas of cooperation between the two nations, the UAE state-run Wam news agency reported, quoting Nigeria’s power minister Joseph Olasunkanmi Tegbe.
