source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/ai/2026/09/gulf-ai-adoption-fast-outpacing-security-controls/

Gulf companies risk exposing sensitive information as employees adopt AI faster than their organisations can put adequate controls in place, an expert has warned. So-called “shadow AI” – where staff use artificial intelligence software without their employer’s knowledge or approval – can expose financial records, personal information and other confidential material to external outlets.
