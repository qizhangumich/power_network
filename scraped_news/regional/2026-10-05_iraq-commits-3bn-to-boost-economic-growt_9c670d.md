# Iraq commits $3bn to boost economic growth
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/economy/2026/10/iraq-commits-3bn-to-boost-economic-growth/

Iraq has allocated ID3.5 trillion dinars ($2.7 billion) to stimulate economic growth, focusing on agriculture and industrialisation, according to Prime Minister Ali Faleh Al-Zaidi. The funding will also support housing, construction, tourism and services through public-private partnerships, and create new employment opportunities, the Iraqi News Agency reported.
