source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/09/us-and-iran-vessel-attacks-drive-up-oil-prices/

Oil prices rose in early trading on Monday after the US and Iran targeted vessels in the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday. Traffic through the waterway fell to its lowest level in three months, according to a maritime vessel-tracking company.
