# Diriyah awards $730m contracts for hotels and branded residences
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/giga-projects/2026/10/diriyah-awards-730m-contracts-for-hotels-and-branded-residences/

Diriyah Company has awarded two construction contracts to build six luxury hotels and 34 branded residences in Diriyah Square, accelerating work on the giga-project. The contracts are worth a combined SAR2.7 billion ($728 million), the Public Investment Fund-owned developer said in a statement.
