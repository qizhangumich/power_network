# Iraq and Qatar to build internet transit route to Europe
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/telecoms/2026/09/iraq-and-ooredoo-to-build-internet-transit-route-to-europe/

Iraq has awarded a contract to Qatari telecoms company Ooredoo to build internet transit capacity to Europe. “Our policy prioritised establishing Iraq as a secure, high-speed internet transit route with low latency,” communications minister Mustafa Sanad told the state-run Iraqi News Agency.
