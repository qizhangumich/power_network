# BlueFive Capital acquires 30% of sports car maker Bugatti
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/finance/2026/09/bluefive-capital-acquires-30-of-sports-car-maker-bugatti/

Abu Dhabi-based alternative asset manager BlueFive Capital has acquired a 30 percent stake in Bugatti, the French sports car maker. The stake was bought from German carmaker Porsche, making the Emirati company the second-largest shareholder after Bugatti Rimac. No financial details were disclosed.
