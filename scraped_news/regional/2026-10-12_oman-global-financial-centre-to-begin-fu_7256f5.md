# Oman global financial centre to begin full operations next year
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/finance/2026/10/oman-global-financial-centre-to-begin-full-operations-next-year/

Oman’s first global financial centre will begin registering companies by the end of 2026 and launch full licencing operations next year, competing with other established GCC hubs.
