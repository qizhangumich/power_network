# Egypt triples oil imports as prices spike
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/09/egypt-triples-oil-imports-as-prices-spike/

Egypt’s oil imports more than tripled in the first half of this year after global crude prices jumped following the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. The third-largest Arab economy also sharply increased liquefied natural gas (LNG) imports as domestic consumption continued to outpace production.
