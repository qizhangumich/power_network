# TotalEnergies commits a further $4bn for Iraq oil projects
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/09/totalenergies-commits-another-4bn-for-iraqs-oil-projects/

French oil major TotalEnergies plans to lift its investment in Iraq by one-third as the country expands its energy sector. The company will increase its investment from $12 billion to $16 billion, the prime minister’s media office reported, quoting chairman Patrick Pouyanné.
