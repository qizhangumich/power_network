# Indian payments giant PhonePe receives approval for UAE
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/banking-finance/2026/09/indian-payments-giant-phonepe-receives-approval-for-uae/

PhonePe, one of the world’s largest digital payments companies, has received in-principle approval from the Central Bank of the UAE to operate in the Emirates.
