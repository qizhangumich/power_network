# UAE suspends Iranian airlines following US sanctions threat
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/aviation/2026/09/uae-suspends-iranian-airlines-following-us-sanctions-threat/

The UAE has suspended all flights operated by Iranian airlines after the US threatened sanctions, according to Iran state media. The US Treasury on September 8 sanctioned 27 airlines and related entities, targeting Iranian carriers and companies that support the country’s aviation sector.
