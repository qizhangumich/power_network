# Standard Chartered brings crypto spot trading to UAE
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/banking-finance/2026/09/standard-chartered-brings-crypto-spot-trading-to-uae/

Standard Chartered has launched a spot trading service in the UAE, allowing its institutional clients in the country to buy and sell Bitcoin and Ether cryptocurrencies. The service, offered through its Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC)-registered branch, makes Standard Chartered the first global bank of its scale to offer spot trading capabilities in the GCC, it claimed […]
