# GCC steel demand ‘deferred’ in 2026 by project award delays
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/industry/2026/10/gcc-steel-demand-deferred-in-2026-by-project-award-delays/

GCC steel demand is expected to reverse sharply in 2026 from the double-digit growth registered in 2025, the World Steel Association (Worldsteel) has said. Demand is forecast down nearly 11 percent this year, driven by delayed construction awards.
