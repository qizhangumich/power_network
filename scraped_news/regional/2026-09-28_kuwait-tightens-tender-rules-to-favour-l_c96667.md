# Kuwait tightens tender rules to favour local SMEs
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/construction/2026/09/kuwait-tightens-tender-rules-to-favour-local-smes/

Foreigners who win public projects in Kuwait must award at least 30 percent of the work to local contractors under an updated law intended to stimulate domestic business and upgrade the performance of national companies.
