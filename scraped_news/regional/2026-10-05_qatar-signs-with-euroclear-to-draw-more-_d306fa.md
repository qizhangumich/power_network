# Qatar signs with Euroclear to draw more global investors
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/banking-finance/2026/10/qatar-signs-with-euroclear-to-draw-more-global-investors/

Qatar is set to establish a new international settlement link with Brussels-based Euroclear for its local government bonds. The move will open its domestic debt market to global investors as it seeks to attract more foreign direct investment.
