# Tabby to expand beyond ‘buy now, pay later’ business
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/banking-finance/2026/09/tabby-to-expand-beyond-buy-now-pay-later-business/

Tabby has raised new funding to expand beyond its “buy now, pay later” business into financial services in Saudi Arabia and the UAE. The Riyadh-based fintech raised $233 million in an equity round led by Hong Kong-based Blue Pool Capital, valuing the company at $6.5 billion, it said in a statement.
