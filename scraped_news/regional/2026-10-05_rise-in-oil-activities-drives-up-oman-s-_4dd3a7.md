# Rise in oil activities drives up Oman’s economy
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/economy/2026/10/rise-in-oil-activities-drives-up-omans-economy/

Oman’s economy grew between April and June this year as disruption to the Strait of Hormuz failed to dampen oil exports from the sultanate.
