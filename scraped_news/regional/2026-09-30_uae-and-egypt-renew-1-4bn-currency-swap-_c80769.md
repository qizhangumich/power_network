# UAE and Egypt renew $1.4bn currency swap agreement
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/banking-finance/2026/09/uae-and-egypt-renew-1-4bn-currency-swap-agreement/

The central banks of the UAE and Egypt have renewed their currency swap agreement for a further five years. The renewed agreement remains worth AED5 billion ($1.4 billion) and is intended to promote bilateral trade and deepen financial cooperation, the UAE state-run Wam news agency reported.
