# Iraq says its oil exports are close to pre-conflict levels
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/09/iraq-says-its-oil-exports-are-close-to-pre-conflict-levels/

Iraq’s crude exports through the Strait of Hormuz have surpassed 3 million barrels per day (bpd) for the first time since the US-Iran conflict erupted in late February, oil minister Bassim Khudair said. Khudair said Iraq’s crude exports were no higher than 200,000 bpd when the government of prime minister Ali Al-Zaidi came to power […]
