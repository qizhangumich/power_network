# Expat remittances from GCC are highest in the world
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/finance/2026/10/expat-remittances-from-gcc-are-highest-in-the-world/

Remittance outflows from Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries were the highest in the world in 2025, rising almost 14 percent year on year.
