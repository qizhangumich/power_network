# She Thought Her Future Was Local. The World Had Other Plans.
source: Arabian Business
url: https://www.arabianbusiness.com/t-magazine/fashion/elina-gunawardena-sri-lankan-model

At 19, Elina Gunawardena has opened Isabel Marant and become the first Sri Lankan model to walk for Victoria’s Secret. Her career offers young women at home a new route to imagine — even as she works out where she wants to go next.
