# QatarEnergy wins seven Brazilian offshore exploration licences
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/10/qatarenergy-wins-seven-brazilian-offshore-exploration-licences/

QatarEnergy, in a consortium with Brazilian state-backed energy company Petrobras, has won seven offshore exploration blocks in northeastern Brazil. The blocks were awarded in the sixth concession bid round held by Brazil’s National Agency of Petroleum, Natural Gas and Biofuels, QatarEnergy said in a statement.
