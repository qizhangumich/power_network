# Flydubai suspends flights to Israel after incident
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/aviation/2026/10/flydubai-temporarily-suspends-flights-to-israel-after-incident/

Flydubai has suspended all flights to and from Israel after a foiled bid to crash a Dubai-Tel Aviv flight on Wednesday. The suspension follows an attempt by a Flydubai co-pilot reportedly to crash the plane carrying 174 passengers.
