# Adia backs buyout of US-based Slate Grocery REIT
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/retail/2026/09/adia-backs-buyout-of-us-based-slate-grocery-reit/

Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (Adia) has backed the acquisition of Canada’s Slate Grocery REIT for $2.3 billion. The purchase will be carried out by New York Stock Exchange-listed shopping centre operator Brixmor Property Group and Everview Partners, a US-based private investment management company.
