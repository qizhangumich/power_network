# Dubai’s GII buys stake in UK fractional property platform
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/real-estate/2026/10/dubais-gii-buys-stake-in-uk-fractional-property-investment-platform/

Gulf Islamic Investments (GII), a Dubai-based sharia-compliant investment company, has acquired a majority stake in London House Exchange, a UK fractional property investment platform. The announcement followed approval from the Financial Conduct Authority, the UK regulator for financial services, LHX said in a statement.
