# Dubai begins driverless transport trial at Bluewaters tourist district
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/transport/2026/10/dubai-begins-driverless-transport-trial-at-bluewaters-tourist-district/

Dubai has begun testing a driverless electric transport system at Bluewaters, exploring ways to link tourist destinations and residential areas to its metro network. The trial forms part of Dubai’s search for cheaper ways to expand public transport without building more rail infrastructure.
