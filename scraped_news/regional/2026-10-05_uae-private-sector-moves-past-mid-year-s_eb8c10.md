# UAE private sector moves past mid-year slowdown
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/economy/2026/10/uae-private-sector-moves-past-mid-year-slowdown/

The non-oil economies of the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Kuwait continued to improve in September despite the ongoing US-Iran war, while the conflict weighed on Qatari businesses, according to PMI reports. Demand conditions in the UAE’s non-oil private sector improved, with output rising at its fastest monthly pace since February, just before war broke out […]
