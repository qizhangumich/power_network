# Gulf suppliers urged to halt Hormuz crossings
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/shipping/2026/10/gulf-suppliers-urged-to-halt-hormuz-crossings/

A maritime security company that advises major Gulf suppliers has urged shipowners to suspend transits through the Strait of Hormuz as Iranian attacks intensify. Marisks, whose risk assessments inform shipping operators and Gulf governments, said Tehran’s forces had changed how they targeted vessels.
