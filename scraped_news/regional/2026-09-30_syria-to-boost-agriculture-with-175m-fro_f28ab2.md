# Syria to boost agriculture with $175m from World Bank
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/agriculture/2026/09/syria-to-boost-agriculture-with-175m-from-world-bank/

Syria has launched a project to increase agricultural output over the next six years, funded by $175 million from the World Bank. The Star programme aims to support crop and livestock production, agricultural co-operatives and private-sector participation, the state-run Sana news agency reported.
