# Iraq tightens public contracts scrutiny in corruption crackdown
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/law/2026/10/iraq-tightens-public-contracts-scrutiny-in-corruption-crackdown/

Iraq is tightening scrutiny of government contracts as part of Prime Minister Ali Al-Zaidi’s anti-corruption drive. The country’s top financial watchdog has introduced additional checks before deals can be finalised.
