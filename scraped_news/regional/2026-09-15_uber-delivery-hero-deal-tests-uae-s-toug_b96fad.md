# Uber-Delivery Hero deal tests UAE’s tougher competition laws
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/law/2026/09/uber-delivery-hero-deal-tests-uaes-tougher-competition-laws/

Uber’s proposed $14.8 billion takeover of German food-ordering company Delivery Hero is being viewed as a test of the UAE’s strengthened competition laws, experts have told AGBI. The deal could signal a tougher era for UAE merger and acquisitions (M&A), with regulators this summer given greater powers to scrutinise market dominance and potentially block transactions. […]
