# Opec Fund allocates $200m for Oman and Morocco projects
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/energy/2026/09/opec-fund-allocates-200m-for-oman-and-morocco-projects/

The Opec Fund for International Development (Opec Fund) has approved $645 million to strengthen infrastructure and drive economic reforms across more than a dozen countries. “These investments address essential foundations of sustainable development: reliable water and energy, resilient infrastructure, effective economic institutions and access to finance,” fund president Abdulhamid Alkhalifa said in a statement.
