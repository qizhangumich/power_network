# US clears Gulf funds to exceed cap in Paramount-Warner deal
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/media/2026/09/us-clears-gulf-funds-to-exceed-cap-in-paramount-warner-deal/

The US Federal Communications Commission has approved Middle Eastern sovereign funds to exceed a foreign-ownership cap in the merged Paramount Skydance-Warner Bros Discovery company. The funds from the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar will indirectly own 49.5 percent of the proposed new entity’s equity without any voting rights.
