# Saudi developer launches $700m fund for second Medina project
source: AGBI

Saudi Arabia’s Knowledge Economic City Company (KEC) is planning to build a new mixed-use project in Medina through a real estate fund. The fund will support the extension of the first phase of the Multaqa Almadinah master development by building Multaqa Almadinah 2 for SAR2.5 billion ($666 million), the company said in a statement.
