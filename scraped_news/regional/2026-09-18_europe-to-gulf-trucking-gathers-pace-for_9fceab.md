# Europe-to-Gulf trucking gathers pace for Aramex
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/logistics/2026/09/trucking-from-europe-to-the-gulf-gains-traction-for-aramex/

Dubai-listed logistics company Aramex is trucking goods from Europe to the Gulf along an overland route that was uneconomical before the Iran war, as businesses seek alternatives to disrupted shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.
