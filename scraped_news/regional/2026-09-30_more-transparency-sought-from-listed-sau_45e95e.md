# More transparency sought from listed Saudi companies
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/markets/2026/09/saudi-arabia-seeks-more-transparency-from-listed-companies/

Companies listed on Saudi Arabia’s main market, the Tadawul, will be required to hold earnings calls twice a year within a set timeframe under draft provisions from the local regulator.
