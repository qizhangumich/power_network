# Oil markets await clarity on US-Iran meeting at UN
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/09/oil-markets-await-clarity-on-us-iran-meeting-at-unga/

Oil prices rose in early trading on Tuesday as investors await clarity on a possible meeting between the US and Iran at the United Nations General Assembly this week.
