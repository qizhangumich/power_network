# Saudi Arabia orders 3,400 railcars in $600m deal
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/transport/2026/09/saudi-arabia-orders-3400-railcars-in-600m-deal/

Saudi Arabia has placed an order with Greenbrier Companies for 3,400 railcars worth $600 million, the New York-listed company said. The orders span a range of carriage types, including 780 railcars for state-run Saudi Arabia Railways (SAR) and tank cars for transporting phosphoric acid and molten sulphur.
