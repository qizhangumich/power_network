# UAE leads Gulf business rebound
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/economy/2026/09/uae-leads-gulf-business-rebound-from-war-disruption/

Non-oil business activity strengthened across the Gulf in August as companies adapted to disruption from the US-Israeli war with Iran, with the sector in the UAE recording its fastest growth in nearly two years.
