# PIF launches real estate company for east coast project
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/real-estate/2026/09/pif-launches-real-estate-company-for-east-coast-project/

Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund (PIF) has launched a real estate company to develop a tourist and residential destination in the country’s Eastern Province. The Gulf Coast Development Company and the private sector will collaborate on the project on the Arabian Gulf’s Al-Khafji coast, in the northern part of the province, a PIF statement said. […]
