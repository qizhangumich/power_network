# UAE commits $45bn to deepen trade ties with Germany
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/trade/2026/09/uae-commits-46bn-to-deepen-trade-ties-with-germany/

The UAE has committed €40 billion ($45 billion) in investments in Germany as part of efforts to build a long-term strategic partnership. The announcement was made during a meeting between UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan and German chancellor Friedrich Merz in Berlin on Thursday, according to a joint declaration.
