# War disruption hits Oman airport passenger traffic
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/aviation/2026/10/war-disrupts-oman-airport-passenger-traffic/

Passenger traffic at Oman’s main airports fell in the first eight months of the year as the US-Iran war disrupted regional aviation and tourism.
