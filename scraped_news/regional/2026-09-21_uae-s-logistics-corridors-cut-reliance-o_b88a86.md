# UAE’s logistics corridors cut reliance on Hormuz
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/logistics/2026/09/uaes-logistics-corridors-cut-reliance-on-hormuz/

New land trade corridors developed in response to the US-Iran war are carrying increasing volumes of goods between the UAE and Oman as businesses adapt to restrictions on shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.
