# Saudi Arabia warns Houthi attacks will not go unpunished
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/10/saudi-warns-houthi-attacks-will-not-go-unpunished-as-airports-hit/

Fighting between Saudi Arabia and Yemeni Houthis has intensified as Riyadh warned that attacks against civilians and civil infrastructure will not go without repercussions. Houthi attacks on Abha Airport and King Khalid International Airport on October 6 and 7 killed three civilians and injured 36, the Saudi aviation regulator said in a social media post. […]
