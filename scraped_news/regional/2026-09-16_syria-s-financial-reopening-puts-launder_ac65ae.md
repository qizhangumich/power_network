# Syria’s financial reopening puts laundering controls to the test
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/banking-finance/2026/09/syrias-financial-reopening-puts-laundering-controls-to-the-test/

Syria’s return to global finance is drawing renewed international scrutiny over its efforts to combat money-laundering and illicit flows, the region’s financial crime watchdog has told AGBI. As foreign investment returns and links with global banks and payment networks are restored, weaknesses in Syria’s financial controls risk giving illicit money greater access to international markets.
