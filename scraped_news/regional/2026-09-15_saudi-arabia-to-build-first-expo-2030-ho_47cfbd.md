# Saudi Arabia to build first Expo 2030 housing project via PPP
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/construction/2026/09/saudi-arabia-to-build-first-expo-2030-housing-project-via-ppp/

Saudi Arabia will build Expo Village, its first residential community in Expo 2030 Riyadh, through a public-private partnership (PPP). The Public Investment Fund-owned Expo 2030 Riyadh and Mohammed Al Habib Real Estate Company signed a SAR3.2 billion ($855 million) joint venture agreement to develop the project.
