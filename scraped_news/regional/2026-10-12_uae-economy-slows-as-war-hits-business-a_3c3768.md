# UAE economy slows as war hits business activity
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/economy/2026/10/uae-economy-slows-as-war-hits-business-activity/

The UAE’s economy contracted in the second quarter of 2026 as non-oil businesses struggled to grow during the US-Iran war. Real GDP, a measure of economic growth adjusted for inflation, fell a little more than 2 percent year on year to AED477 billion ($130 billion) in the quarter ended June 30.
