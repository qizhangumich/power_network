# Asyad Shipping expands tanker order with Hyundai
source: AGBI

Asyad Shipping, a subsidiary of Oman’s state-backed Asyad Group, has awarded a contract to South Korean shipbuilder Hyundai Heavy Industries to build two medium-range tankers.
