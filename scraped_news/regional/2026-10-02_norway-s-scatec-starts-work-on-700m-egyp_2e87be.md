# Norway’s Scatec starts work on $700m Egyptian wind farm
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/renewable-energy/2026/10/norways-scatec-starts-work-on-700m-egyptian-wind-farm/

Norwegian renewable energy provider Scatec has begun work on the 900MW Shadwan onshore wind project near Egypt’s Red Sea coast. Scatec secured a $150 million equity bridge loan from the Arab Energy Fund before construction began.
