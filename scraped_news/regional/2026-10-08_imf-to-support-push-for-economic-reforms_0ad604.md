# IMF to support push for economic reforms in war-torn Yemen
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/economy/2026/10/imf-to-support-push-for-economic-reforms-in-war-torn-yemen/

The International Monetary Fund is providing Yemen with non-financial support to bolster its public finances after the country became an active front in the Iran conflict. The 18-month programme aims to help Yemen’s internationally recognised government “preserve macroeconomic stability… reinforce fiscal discipline and maintain a prudent monetary policy stance”, the IMF said on Wednesday.
