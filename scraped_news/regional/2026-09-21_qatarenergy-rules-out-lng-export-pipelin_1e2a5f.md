# QatarEnergy rules out LNG export pipelines
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/09/qatarenergy-rules-out-lng-export-pipelines/

State-backed QatarEnergy will not build pipelines to transport its liquefied natural gas (LNG) through neighbouring countries and will instead rely primarily on the Strait of Hormuz, its CEO said. “We have decided not to go with pipelines.
