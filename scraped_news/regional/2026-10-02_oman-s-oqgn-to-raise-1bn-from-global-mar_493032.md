# Oman’s OQGN to raise $1bn from global markets
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/10/oman-gas-company-oqgn-to-raise-1bn-from-global-markets/

Oman’s state-controlled OQ Gas Networks (OQGN) hope to raise $1 billion from global capital markets to diversify its funding sources. The funding will be through the issuance of sukuk – Islamic bonds – and is planned to be listed on the London Stock Exchange, the company said in a filing on the Muscat Stock Exchange. […]
