# Saudi Arabia doubles passenger train order with Swiss company
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/transport/2026/10/saudi-arabia-doubles-passenger-train-order-with-swiss-company/

Saudi Arabia Railways (SAR) has exercised its option to buy 10 intercity passenger trains from Stadler, a Swiss rail infrastructure and train manufacturer, as it seeks to strengthen the inland rail and road network amid the escalating regional conflict.
