# Qatar opens tenders for $39bn infrastructure projects
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/infrastructure/2026/09/qatar-opens-tenders-for-39bn-infrastructure-projects/

Qatar has started tendering multi-billion-dollar infrastructure projects as public-private partnerships over the next five years. The new infrastructure projects are worth $38.5 billion, with the initial tendering processes commencing immediately, the state-run Qatar News Agency reported, quoting prime minister Sheikh Mohammed Jassim Al-Thani at the Qatar Economic Forum.
