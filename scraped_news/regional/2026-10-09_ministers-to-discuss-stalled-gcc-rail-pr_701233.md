# Ministers to discuss stalled GCC rail project
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/infrastructure/2026/10/ministers-to-discuss-stalled-gcc-rail-project/

GCC transport ministers are due to meet on November 8 to discuss advancing a regulatory framework for the region’s long-delayed rail network, as shipping disruption adds urgency to plans for better transit links between the six member states.
