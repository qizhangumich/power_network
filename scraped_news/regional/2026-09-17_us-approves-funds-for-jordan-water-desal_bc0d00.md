# US approves funds for Jordan water desalination
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/infrastructure/2026/09/us-approves-funds-for-jordans-water-desalination-project/

Jordan will receive US funding to build critical infrastructure, in particular a $6 billion national desalination project that aims to ease acute water scarcity. The US International Development Finance Corporation (DFC) said in a statement that it had approved more than $8 billion in new investments for Jordan, Africa and Ukraine.
