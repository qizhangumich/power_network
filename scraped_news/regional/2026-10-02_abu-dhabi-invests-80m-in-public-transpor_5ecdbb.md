# Abu Dhabi invests $80m in public transport technology
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/transport/2026/10/abu-dhabi-invests-80m-in-public-transport-technology/

Abu Dhabi is investing around AED300 million ($80 million) to upgrade the technology used to manage its public transport network and collect fares.
