# Oman to redevelop Sultan Qaboos Port waterfront
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/tourism/2026/10/oman-to-redevelop-sultan-qaboos-port-waterfront/

Oman plans to redevelop the Sultan Qaboos Port waterfront in Muscat into a major tourism and leisure destination. The sultanate is seeking to expand its tourism sector despite a regional decline in visitor numbers caused by the US-Iran war.
