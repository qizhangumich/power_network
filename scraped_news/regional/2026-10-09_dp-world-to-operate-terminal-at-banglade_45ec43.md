# DP World to operate terminal at Bangladesh’s largest port
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/logistics/2026/10/dp-world-to-operate-terminal-at-bangladeshs-largest-port/

Dubai’s DP World has signed a 15-year concession to operate and maintain a container terminal at Bangladesh’s largest port, supporting the country’s expanding global trade. The agreement with state-owned Chittagong Port Authority (CPA) forms part of a public-private partnership initiative agreed by the Bangladeshi and Dubai governments in 2019.
