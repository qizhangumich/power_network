# Saudi Arabia expands pipelines to reduce reliance on Hormuz

Saudi Arabia’s energy minister has outlined plans to expand the kingdom’s pipeline capacity to protect its oil exports from future disruptions to shipping routes. Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman said upgrading the East-West pipeline and temporarily converting gas pipelines to carry crude would give the country more export options.
