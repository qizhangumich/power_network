# Saudi oil exports from eastern ports climb
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/09/saudi-oil-exports-through-gulf-climb/

Saudi Arabia has increased oil exports from its eastern ports since the suspension of the East-West pipeline, according to satellite observations.
