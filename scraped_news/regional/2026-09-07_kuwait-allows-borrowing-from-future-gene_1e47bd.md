# Kuwait allows borrowing from Future Generations Fund
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/economy/2026/09/kuwait-allows-borrowing-from-future-generations-fund/

Kuwait’s cabinet has allowed the government to borrow from the country’s Future Generations Fund (FGF) for the first time since the facility’s inception in 1976, with a view to supporting the state budget.
