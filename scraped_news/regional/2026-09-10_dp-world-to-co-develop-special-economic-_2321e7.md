# DP World to co-develop special economic zone in Kenya
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/industry/2026/09/dp-world-to-co-develop-special-economic-zone-in-kenya/

Dubai’s DP World has signed a deal to co-develop a 222-hectare special economic zone (SEZ) in Kenya as it expands its footprint in Africa. The Mombasa Industrial Park will be developed with Kenya-based investment group GulfCap Africa.
