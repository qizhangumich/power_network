# Aramco drops oil price for Asian buyers
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/10/aramco-drops-oil-price-for-asian-buyers/

Saudi Aramco will sell oil to Asian markets next month at its highest discount since Covid. The Saudi state-owned energy company is offering an average cut of $5.24 per barrel against Oman and Dubai benchmarks, according to its pricing sheet for November.
