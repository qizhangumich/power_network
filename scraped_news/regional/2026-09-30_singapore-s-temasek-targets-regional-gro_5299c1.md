# Singapore’s Temasek targets regional growth with Gulf offices
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/finance/2026/09/singapores-temasek-targets-regional-growth-with-gulf-offices/

Singaporean state-owned Temasek plans to open offices in the UAE and Saudi Arabia in the first half of 2027, boosting its presence in the home of some of the world’s largest sovereign funds.
