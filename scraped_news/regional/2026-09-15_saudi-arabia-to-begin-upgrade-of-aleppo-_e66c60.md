# Saudi Arabia to begin upgrade of Aleppo airport early next year
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/aviation/2026/09/saudi-arabia-to-begin-upgrade-of-aleppo-airport-early-next-year/

Saudi Arabia will begin upgrading Aleppo International Airport early next year as part of efforts to revive Syria’s tourism sector. Riyadh committed SAR7.5 billion ($2 billion) to developing two airports in Aleppo, Khalid Al Falih, then minister for investment, said in February.
