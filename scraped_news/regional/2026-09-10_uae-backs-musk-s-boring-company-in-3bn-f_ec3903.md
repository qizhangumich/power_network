# UAE backs Musk’s Boring Company in $3bn fundraising
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/transport/2026/09/uae-backs-musks-boring-company-in-3bn-fundraising/

Elon Musk’s Boring Company has raised $3 billion in a funding round led by the UAE and affiliated investment entities. The money will accelerate development of large-scale underground infrastructure across the UAE, including more than 150km of tunnels, building on the previously announced Dubai Loop project, the company said in a statement.
