# Nvidia shares surge over 30% as Jensen Huang returns as CEO following foreign intelligence concerns
source: Arabian Business
url: https://www.arabianbusiness.com/abnews/nvidia-shares-surge-over-30-as-jensen-huang-returns-as-ceo-following-foreign-intelligence-concerns

Nvidia shares surged more than 30% in premarket trading on Monday after the chipmaking giant reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive officer, triggering a broader rally in technology stocks and boosting investor confidence in the artificial intelligence sector.
