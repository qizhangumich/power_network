# Adia cuts stake in Indian eyewear company Lenskart
source: AGBI

Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (Adia) has cut its holding in Lenskart, an Indian eyewear retailer, selling 35 million shares in a block deal worth INR23.9 billion ($250 million).
