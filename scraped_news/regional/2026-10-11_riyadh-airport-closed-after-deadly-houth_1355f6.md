# Riyadh airport closed after deadly Houthi missile strike
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/aviation/2026/10/riyadh-airport-closed-after-deadly-houthi-missile-strike/

Saudi Arabia’s largest airport remains closed after the deadliest attack on the country in more than a decade. At least 12 people were killed and more than 300 were injured, some in “critical condition”, in a Houthi missile attack on King Khalid Airport in Riyadh on Saturday, Saudi authorities said.
