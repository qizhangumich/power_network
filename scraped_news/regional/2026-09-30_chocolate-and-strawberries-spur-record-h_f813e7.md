# Chocolate and strawberries spur record-high Egypt food exports
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/trade/2026/09/chocolate-and-strawberries-spur-record-high-egypt-food-exports/

Egypt’s food exports hit a record high in the first eight months of this year after chocolate sales doubled and Saudi Arabia boosted imports from the North African country.
