# Qatar and JPMorgan to launch $20bn investment partnership
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/banking-finance/2026/09/qatar-and-jpmorgan-to-launch-20bn-investment-partnership/

Qatar Investment Authority (QIA) has signed an agreement with US-based JPMorgan Asset Management (JPMAM) to invest billions of dollars across public and private markets. Under the partnership JPMAM will manage a customised portfolio for QIA and invest $15 billion in public equities to support the fund’s long-term investment objectives, the state-run Qatar News Agency reported.
