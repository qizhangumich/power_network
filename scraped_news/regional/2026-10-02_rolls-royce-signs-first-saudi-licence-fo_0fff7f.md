# Rolls-Royce signs first Saudi licence for high-speed engines
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/manufacturing/2026/10/rolls-royce-signs-first-saudi-licence-for-high-speed-engines/

Saudi Engines Manufacturing Company (Makeen), an industrial venture backed by Saudi Aramco, is to partner with the UK’s Rolls-Royce to manufacture high-speed engines in Saudi Arabia. The individual licence agreement signed by the companies represents the first such deal in the kingdom.
