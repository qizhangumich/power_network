# Saudi Arabia contracts SLB to drill 450 wells
source: AGBI
url: https://www.agbi.com/oil-and-gas/2026/09/saudi-arabia-contracts-slb-to-drill-450-wells/

Saudi Arabia has awarded multinational oilfield services company SLB four integrated well construction contracts. SLB, formerly known as Schlumberger, will manage construction services, delivering 450 wells, the company said.
