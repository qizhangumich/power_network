# World’s top LNG buyer sees Qatar supply disruption persisting into winter
source: The National
url: https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/10/02/worlds-top-lng-buyer-sees-qatar-supply-disruption-persisting-into-winter/

(Bloomberg) -- The head of the world’s largest liquefied natural gas buyer expects disruptions to the Strait of Hormuz to persist, further curtailing flows from a region that accounted for about a fifth of global supply before the Middle East war.
