# Qatar extends LNG force majeure as Hormuz disruption threatens winter supply
source: The National
url: https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/09/28/qatar-extends-lng-force-majeure-as-hormuz-disruption-threatens-winter-supply/

(Bloomberg) -- Qatar has extended force majeure on liquefied natural gas shipments to Asia and Europe by another month as disruptions continue to hamper energy flows through the critical Strait of Hormuz.
