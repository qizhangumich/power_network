# Huawei found guilty of money laundering and conspiracy to steal trade secrets in US case
source: The National
url: https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/11/25/huawei-found-guilty-of-money-laundering-and-conspiracy-to-steal-trade-secrets-in-us-case/

(Bloomberg) -- Huawei Technologies Co., China’s largest technology company, was found guilty of multiple US criminal charges, including money laundering, a conspiracy to steal trade secrets and defrauding banks about its business in Iran and North Korea.
