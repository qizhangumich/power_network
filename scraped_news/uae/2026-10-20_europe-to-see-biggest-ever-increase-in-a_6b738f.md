# Europe to see 'biggest-ever' increase in air fares next year, Ryanair chief says
source: The National
url: https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/aviation/2026/10/20/europe-to-see-biggest-ever-increase-in-air-fares-next-year-ryanair-chief-says/

(Bloomberg) -- Ryanair Holdings Plc Chief Executive Officer Michael O’Leary predicted a record increase in European air fares next year as the Middle East war continues to drive up oil prices, forcing airlines to pass on their fuel costs to consumers.
