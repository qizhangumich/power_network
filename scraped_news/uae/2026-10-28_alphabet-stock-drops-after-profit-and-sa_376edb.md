# Alphabet stock drops after profit and sales fall
source: The National
url: https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/us/2026/10/28/alphabet-stock-drops-after-profit-and-sales-fall/

(Bloomberg) -- Alphabet Inc. reported quarterly sales and profit in the third quarter that fell short of expectations, a sign that the firm is failing to generate enough return to justify its massive investments in AI. The stock fell in late trading.
